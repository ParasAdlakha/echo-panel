3 · What we measure

  • ρ|y: correlation between advisors once the outcome is known (what “they agree, so it's corroborated” assumes is zero)
  • neff = 5 / (1 + 4ρ): effective number of independent advisors
  • Corroboration premium: Brier of “five independent witnesses” minus Brier of the plain average
  • AI reader test: a decision-maker model reads the panel with no note, a generic warning, the true independence label, or a false “5.0 independent” label
Temperature 1 for all advisors. Every call is cached and replayable. Bootstrap 95% CIs over questions; all panels compared on the same questions.
