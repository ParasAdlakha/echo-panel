The problem

Agreement is evidence only if the advisors are independent

  • A second expert who agrees adds evidence only if her opinion did not come from the first (Goldman 2001).
  • Dependent sources carry far less information, with a hard ceiling (Clemen & Winkler 1985).
  • Language models share data, recipes and retrieved documents, and make similar mistakes (Kim et al. 2025; Goel et al. 2025).
  • People treat unanimous AI agents as social proof (Lee & Lee 2026).

Result 4

Machines average. People count.

Two AI decision-makers read every panel with no note, a generic warning, a true independence label, or a false "5.0 independent" label.

They stayed at the panel average (median 0.6 points away), even on 900+ unanimous panels. No note changed their accuracy.

The over-counting risk sits with people.

What it means

Show the opinions, not the voices

  • Display neff, not n. Five advisors on screen overstate the evidence three to five times.
  • Personas are presentation. Don't label them as independent experts.
  • Collect before they talk. Discussion erased the independence that model diversity bought.
  • An auditable independence label. One number from a public track record, issued by someone other than the vendor. Human-study protocol preregistered in the report.
